Before you save

  • Read the full meter total, including decimal digits shown.
  • Record the actual date/time of the reading.
  • If a meter has been replaced or reset, record it in notes and check the lower-reading warning.
  • Keep photos small; they are saved locally in this browser.

Choose a selectable-text PDF for automatic extraction. All extraction happens in your browser. Scanned PDFs and photos can be attached, but may require manual entry if the text cannot be read.

What will be imported?

  • Actual bill period and invoice date.
  • Both meters’ previous and current readings where found.
  • Usage charges and bill total.
  • Tariff line details and the next meter-reading window when visible.
  • Two meter observations dated to the bill-period start and end.

Imported meter readings are tagged with the invoice source. If a matching reading already exists, the app will link it rather than create a duplicate.

Tariff rates

Reference rates are prefilled from Yarra Valley Water's 2026–27 published pricing page. Confirm the rate and tariff treatment against your actual bill before relying on forecasts.

Mains reference values: $3.8313, $5.0268 and $5.8403/kL at published 0–440, 441–880 and 881+ litres/day steps. Recycled reference: $2.0133/kL. Fixed charges are left at $0 until entered from your bill.
